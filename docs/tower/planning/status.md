### What works today

- **A whole match**, in real browsers: create a room, share `/r/CODE`, join (name only, a bobble-hat avatar is generated), pick crews, set the rules (line, drawing time, daze, steals), start. Both crews draw at once. The towers take most of the screen; the middle has your crew's drawing over a play card that flips between the drawing tools and the guess line with the placing controls (no guess log or chat). The other crew's drawing hangs small in their tower's sky. One guess line serves both drawings: your crew's is checked first, then the other crew's (a steal). The letter mask only shows at a third of the round. The drawer has a paint bucket that only colours in closed shapes, and closing a shape with the brush pops a swatch of seven colours over it: one tap colours it in (2026-10-20). The drawer (or the thief, for a steal) chooses 🧱 Keep & place or 🌀 Curse (K / C): a kept piece is theirs to place, a cursed one is placed by the other crew (←/→ move, ↑/W rotate clockwise, Q/E either way, ↓, Space, or the buttons on the play card). Fumbles daze, topping out wins, then the results (awards, everyone's numbers) and a rematch.
- **One profile with every game** (2026-10-21, [decisions.md](../../technical/decisions.md#d13--one-profile-for-every-game-a-portal-a-shared-guest-and-an-account-widget) D13): your name and drawing are shared with DoodleWhirl and the games portal (`lib/identity.ts` over `@pictiotheme/account`). The **account widget** is in the top-left corner of every screen (`features/account`): home, the waiting room's header, the results, and just your avatar during a match (the score bar moved over for it). Its editor works in a room too: a new name or drawing reaches everyone's crew list (`PUT /api/tower/rooms/:code/me`, tower-core's `identity` event). On the home page the name field follows the profile until you type, and a typed name is saved after a short pause. The old `doodle-tower.identity` in browser storage moves into the profile once.
- **A playful home page** (2026-10-21, [screens.md](../design/screens.md#1-home)): a logo whose O's are balloons you can pop and whose T is the plinth; your avatar on a little plinth with **✏️ Draw yourself** (the game's drawing board; the doodle is cut out like a block and kept in this browser) and your name; and one balloon bunch carrying the three ways in, swaying slowly and lifting off into the sky when you pick one. Its balloons pop: pop them all and the menu falls into the village, squashing villagers, who clean it up and then build it again. No explanations: **How to play?** opens three panels instead.
- **Ways in, like DoodleWhirl's** (2026-10-20): **Quick game** (the fullest public room with space, waiting rooms first, or a new public one), **Private game** (a code or a pasted invite link) and **Create a lobby** (private). The waiting room's invite card shows the room's **share card**, the code, *Copy invite link* and the host's 🔒/🌍 switch (`isPublic` in the settings). An invite link pasted in Discord, Slack, WhatsApp… unfurls into the card: Caddy sends link-preview bots to `/api/tower/rooms/:code/embed` (Open Graph tags), and the server draws the card in the Sunny meadow look (`tower-share-card.ts`, Lilita One, rasterised with resvg like DoodleWhirl's).
- **Waiting room on one screen** (2026-10-21): crews and rules on the left, the invite and the start button on the right, no scrolling from 1024×768 up. Rules use game-styled switches (a block knob that springs across) and sliding pickers (`ui/Switch.tsx`, `ui/Segmented.tsx`). The share card's sign now says "Come build a tower with us!" and hangs from two bunches of balloons (card revision 2). *Copy the card* is gone: the invite link unfurls into it.
- **Curses replace Send** (2026-10-21, stage 1a of [power-ups.md](power-ups.md)): every piece is made with a rolled curse, shown on the decide card's 🌀 Curse button: 🧊 Slippery (friction 0.05, the lower friction of a contact wins) or ❄️ Big ice (1.4× the area, and slippery). A cursed piece goes to the other crew with its curse; frost shows it on the tower, and the banner names it. The host can turn curses off (Curse is then the old Send). The sandbox has a curse picker under "Your block". The Meanest curse award replaces Meanest send.
- **Spells, golden words and comeback charges** (2026-10-21, stage 1b): a crew holds up to two spells, shown in its tower's header, and casts them at the other tower (1 / 2, or the buttons) after a 2s warning: 🌫️ Fog hides the top of their tower from its placer for 10s; 🐦‍⬛ Magpie takes the next piece queued for them, for the caster to place: a black-and-white magpie flies in and circles their tower, carries the piece off and flies it in to the thief's balloons. Spells come from golden words (about 1 in 6, in gold for both crews) and comeback charges (a piece falling off, or 2 m behind for 20s; one per 90s). The sandbox can show the fog.
- **Sky stars and Shaky hand** (2026-10-21, stage 2): a minute in and every 90s, a ⭐ star appears just past the edge of each tower's platform, the same place for both; a settled piece of the tower that touches it for 3s catches it, for a spell. 〰️ Shaky hand, a third spell, makes the other drawer's new lines wobble for 10s (the server and the drawer's client wobble them the same way). The sandbox has a star to practise catching. Short fuse was too weak (it handed over a free piece) and was removed on 2026-10-21, to come back reworked.
- **Melt, Bouncy, Blob and double curses** (2026-10-21, stage 3): 🫠 Melt makes the piece droop in the middle like a candle (top dips, bottom bulges, corners round off), doodle and all; 🏀 Bouncy bounces until 3s after it lands; 🍮 Blob is bigger and melted. A golden word's piece rolls two curses that don't clash. All of them are in the sandbox's curse picker.
- **The Pigeon** (2026-10-21, stage 4): a fourth spell. A pigeon lands on the outer edge of the other tower's top piece, a small weight that tips it, until a new piece scares it away (or 25s pass). It grips, doesn't count for height, flies in to land and flies off when it goes. The sandbox can land one. Made a little heavier after testing (0.3 → 0.4 kg). The sandbox can send a magpie too, and both birds have wing sounds coming and going (a magpie that gives up caws as it leaves).
- **Test mode** (2026-10-21, [test-mode.md](test-mode.md)): try every power alone. **🧪 Test the powers** on the home page (development only) makes a room where bots fill the crews and draw coloured-in rectangles; you press **Guess ours** / **Steal theirs** instead of typing, then keep or curse and place as in a match. A test panel picks the next piece's curse, makes the rival bot solve, steal, curse at you or cast, gives either crew any spell, makes drawings golden and brings the stars; its checklist lists what to try and what to look for. The server refuses test rooms in production (`TOWER_TEST_MODE`).
- **Power sounds** (2026-10-21): every power has a synthesised sound: a glittery run for a golden word, chimes for spells gained and stars caught, a whoosh to cast and a "dun-dun-dun" when one's cast at you, then each spell's own (fog's whoosh, the magpie's caws, Shaky hand's warble, the pigeon's coo and its wings), and a boing, squelch or glassy tink when a cursed piece lands on your tower. The sandbox plays them too.
- **The "Sunny meadow" look** (2026-10-20, [visual-identity.md](../design/visual-identity.md)): a sky with drifting clouds, hills and a village on every page; each tower on a grassy stone plinth, climbing past the clouds; the piece being placed floats under balloons; bunting for the line. Sprites are Kenney's CC0 packs in `apps/tower-web/public/scenery/`.
- **A living village** (2026-10-20, [visual-identity.md](../design/visual-identity.md#villagers)): villagers busy at jobs (chatting, hammering, watering, sweeping, carrying crates, watching) and passers-by coming and going. On a tower, a piece that falls off lands on the lane with a thump: neighbours jump, the closest one breaks it up with a mallet, and anyone it lands on is squashed flat, waddles about squeaking, then pops back. Each player's own, cosmetic. The first **sounds** go with it (thump, whacks, synthesised yelps, squish, squeaks, pop), heard even when the ground is out of view.
- **Music and game sounds** (2026-10-20, [visual-identity.md](../design/visual-identity.md#sound)): OwlishMedia's CC0 "Happy Clappy Loop" as the main theme on every page, and synthesised jingles for the match: your turn to draw or place, right/close/wrong guesses, steals both ways, a piece sent your way, placing thud, fumble wah-wah, topping out, the last seconds' ticks, win/lose/draw. A sound control top right on every page: on/off, music and effects volumes.
- **`packages/tower-core`**: the pure match state machine, 40 tests covering every rule, including privacy (the word only to the drawer, the mask and the guesses only to the crew).
- **Server `tower` module**: rooms, routes, the `/ws/tower` socket, physics in a pool of worker threads (frames at 20 Hz). Its tests play a round over real sockets, through the worker, against the real database.
- **Data**: `tower_decks` (seeded with six curated decks: Tower Mix is 920 easy cards), `tower_matches`, `tower_match_players` (migration 0012).
- **`apps/tower-web`**: home, waiting room, game screen, results, plus the Phase 0 sandbox. The placer sees their piece move as they press keys (local prediction); everyone else sees it through interpolated frames.
- **Deployment prepared, not done**: a second Caddy site block (`TOWER_SITE_ADDRESS`, `TOWER_TLS_DIRECTIVE`), the tower build in the web image and the deploy workflow, `TOWER_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` for the server. `caddy validate` passes.

## How to run and verify

```sh
pnpm db:up && pnpm --filter @pictiotheme/server db:migrate   # migration 0012 + the tower allowlist
pnpm --filter @pictiotheme/server dev                         # API on :3000
pnpm dev:tower                                                # http://localhost:4322
pnpm check                                                    # everything (server tests need db:up)
pnpm --filter @pictiotheme/tower-web test:e2e                 # home, sandbox, a 4-player round, test mode (needs the API)
```

If port 3000 is taken, run the API elsewhere and point the site at it: `PORT=3100 pnpm --filter @pictiotheme/server dev` and `TOWER_API_PORT=3100 pnpm dev:tower` (the e2e test then too).

To play a match alone, open four browser profiles (or windows in private mode): each one is a different guest. To try the powers alone, use **🧪 Test the powers** on the home page instead ([test-mode.md](test-mode.md)).

## Known gaps

- **The drawn avatar isn't shared with DoodleWhirl yet.** The two sites are on different domains, so neither can read the other's browser storage. Plan: "Play Doodle Tower →" / "Play DoodleWhirl →" links that carry your name and drawing after the `#`, and a "Use this name and drawing?" confirm on arrival. Later, shared accounts (Better Auth). The image format is already the same (a 128×128 PNG with a clear background).
- **The home page's logo entrance** (the logo floating down under balloons and landing on its plinth) and **a popped O falling into the village** for a villager to clean up are ideas for later.
- **The fallen menu is away for 15–20s.** Most of it is the clean-up: villagers only answer once they've stopped staring. If it's too long in practice, let gawkers be called, or break the planks into fewer bits.
- **E2E runs with reduced motion** (`playwright.config.ts`): the swaying menu would never be still enough for Playwright to click.

- **Reconnecting sends every piece's strokes** in the snapshot (to draw them). Fine for a match, heavy if a tower grows very tall; later the server could send each piece's bitmap once.
- **A physics worker that crashes isn't restarted**: its rooms' towers freeze (it's logged). Restart and rebuild the worlds from the pieces if it ever happens.
- **No moderation yet** for drawings (anyone can draw anything) or for writing the word instead of drawing it ([open-questions.md](open-questions.md)).
- **Phones** get a "bigger screen" notice in the room; the home page works anywhere.
- **The DoodleWhirl Playwright suite** still needs a run where port 3000 is free, to confirm the canvas extraction and the shared socket code end to end (its server tests pass).
